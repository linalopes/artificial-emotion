## About

The Open Research Studio is a public event to share ongoing experiments, prototypes and conversations around the research of Artificial Emotion.

Visitors are invited to explore current work, see prototypes in development and talk about themes such as movement, soft mechanisms, sensing and bodily signals. The event is open to students, researchers, collaborators and anyone interested in the project.
