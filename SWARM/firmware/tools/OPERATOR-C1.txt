SWARM Phase C.1 — operator cheat sheet (Mau02–Mau09)
=====================================================

Prerequisites
- Hardware coordinator already running on :8787 (fleet=hardware). Do not start another.
- Mau01 already commissioned and recorded in tools/.local/commissioning_manifest.json
- Wi-Fi password will be prompted once and stored under tools/.local/ (gitignored)

Commands (from SWARM/firmware)
  python3 tools/commission_next.py status
  python3 tools/commission_next.py inspect

Mau02 (new ESP32 on USB — not Mau01)
  python3 tools/commission_next.py commission --module Mau02 --coordinator <mac-lan-ip>

Then Mau03–Mau09
  1. Unplug the board you just finished
  2. Plug the next ESP32-S3
  3. python3 tools/commission_next.py next

The tool will ask before flashing, refuse to re-id Mau01, reuse local secrets,
verify Wi-Fi + coordinator registration, and update the local MAC manifest.

No homing / no motor moves during this phase.
